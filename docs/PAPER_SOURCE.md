An independent selected-statement crosswalk read the full existence/uniqueness,
growth and exponential-data proposition blocks, plus the explicit resolvent,
H and Appendix A.1 blocks in both sources. Constants, hypotheses and branches
used by all 16 selected Challenge statements agree. This is a statement-level
crosswalk, not a claim that the complete proof texts are identical.

Verified public locations: Proposition 2.2 and displays (2.7)–(2.9); display
(2.18); Theorem 2.4; Theorem 2.12; Proposition 2.14; Appendix Lemma A.1.
The scalar summability target additionally includes its proved zero-time endpoint.
The printed Appendix A.1 allows nonzero b; this formalization proves its positive
b form and separately refutes the specified negative-b case.
